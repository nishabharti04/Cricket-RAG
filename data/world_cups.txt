2011 Cricket World Cup

The 2011 Cricket World Cup was hosted by India, Sri Lanka and Bangladesh.
India won the tournament by defeating Sri Lanka in the final.
The final was played at Wankhede Stadium in Mumbai.
India scored 277 runs for the loss of 4 wickets.
MS Dhoni scored an unbeaten 91 runs and was named Player of the Match.
Gautam Gambhir scored 97 runs.
Yuvraj Singh was named Player of the Tournament.

2023 Cricket World Cup

The 2023 Cricket World Cup was hosted by India.
Australia won the tournament by defeating India in the final.
The final was played at Narendra Modi Stadium in Ahmedabad.
Virat Kohli was named Player of the Tournament.
Rohit Sharma was India's captain.
Virat Kohli scored 765 runs during the tournament.

1983 Cricket World Cup

The 1983 Cricket World Cup was held in England.
India won the tournament by defeating West Indies in the final.
Kapil Dev was the captain of India.
India defeated West Indies in the final at Lord's.
