Indian Cricket Players

Virat Kohli is an Indian international cricketer and batsman.
Virat Kohli was named Player of the Tournament in the 2023 Cricket World Cup.
Virat Kohli scored 765 runs during the 2023 Cricket World Cup.

Rohit Sharma is an Indian international cricketer and batsman.
Rohit Sharma was India's captain during the 2023 Cricket World Cup.

MS Dhoni is an Indian wicketkeeper-batsman.
MS Dhoni was India's captain in limited-overs cricket.
MS Dhoni scored an unbeaten 91 runs in the 2011 Cricket World Cup final.

Gautam Gambhir is an Indian former international cricketer.
Gautam Gambhir scored 97 runs in the 2011 Cricket World Cup final.

Yuvraj Singh is an Indian former international cricketer.
Yuvraj Singh was named Player of the Tournament in the 2011 Cricket World Cup.

Kapil Dev was the captain of India when India won the 1983 Cricket World Cup.

Sachin Tendulkar is one of India's most prominent international batsmen.

Sunil Gavaskar was one of India's prominent opening batsmen.
